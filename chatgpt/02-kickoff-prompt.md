# セッション開始プロンプト（毎回これをコピペ → その後マイクボタン）

音声モードは Project 指示を無視することがあるため、**毎回テキストでこれを送ってから**
音声に切り替えるのが最も確実です。

---

## テンプレート（{{ }} を差し替え）

```
SESSION TYPE: {{OPINION}}
TOPIC: {{topic}}
TARGET PHRASES (try to make me use these naturally, but do not tell me you are doing it):
- {{phrase 1}}
- {{phrase 2}}
- {{phrase 3}}
MY KNOWN WEAK POINTS (watch for these in the report):
- {{前回のレポートから転記}}

Rules: English only. No corrections until I say END SESSION. Natural speed, C1 vocabulary,
do not simplify. Your turns: 2-4 sentences, always end with a question. Make me do 70% of
the talking. Push back on my position at least twice. If I go silent, offer one phrase and
wait — do not fill the silence yourself.

Start now with one short line and your first question.
```

---

## 記入例

```
SESSION TYPE: BUSINESS
TOPIC: I have to tell a client that the delivery will slip by three weeks. You are the client.
TARGET PHRASES:
- I want to be upfront with you about...
- What I'd propose is...
- I take your point, but...
MY KNOWN WEAK POINTS:
- I overuse "I think" instead of hedging properly
- I default to simple past instead of present perfect

Rules: English only. No corrections until I say END SESSION. Natural speed, C1 vocabulary,
do not simplify. Your turns: 2-4 sentences, always end with a question. Make me do 70% of
the talking. Push back on my position at least twice. If I go silent, offer one phrase and
wait — do not fill the silence yourself.

Start now with one short line and your first question.
```
